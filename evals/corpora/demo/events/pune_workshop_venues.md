# Pune Workshop Venue Guide

This guide lists the venues approved for customer workshops, partner briefings and internal offsites in Pune. Bookings are made through the Events Desk. Capacities below are for classroom seating unless stated otherwise.

## Riverside Hall, Koregaon Park

Riverside Hall is the preferred venue for customer workshops of 20 to 40 people. The main hall seats 40 people in classroom layout and 32 people in boardroom layout. It has two breakout rooms that each seat 8 people.

Riverside Hall provides a built-in projector, two wireless microphones and video-conferencing equipment. Wi-Fi is included in the room rate. The standard room rate is INR 58,000 per day, which includes the main hall and one breakout room.

Riverside Hall operates an in-house kitchen and does not allow outside caterers. Parking is available for 25 cars.

## Baner Tech Hub

Baner Tech Hub is a flexible workspace suited to hands-on technical workshops. The Innovation Room seats 30 people in classroom layout and 24 people at lab benches with power at every seat.

The standard room rate is INR 42,000 per day. Whiteboards and a 75-inch display are included; a projector must be requested at least 3 working days in advance.

Baner Tech Hub has no in-house kitchen. Food must be arranged through an approved external caterer from the vendor directory.

## Hinjewadi Convention Suites

Hinjewadi Convention Suites is used for large events. The Grand Suite seats 120 people theatre-style and 70 people in classroom layout. It is not recommended for groups smaller than 50 because the minimum billing is for 50 attendees.

The standard rate is INR 1,450 per attendee per day, including a working lunch and two tea breaks.

## Booking lead times

Venues must be booked at least 10 working days before the event. Requests made with less notice need approval from the Events Desk lead and may incur a short-notice surcharge of 15 percent of the room rate.

A booking is only confirmed once the Events Desk issues a confirmation number. Holds without a confirmation number lapse after 48 hours.
