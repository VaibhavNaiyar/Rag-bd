# Laptop Security Policy

## Encryption

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Devices without encryption are blocked from the corporate network after 24 hours.

## Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 2 hours. IT will remotely wipe the device. A police report is required for stolen devices before a replacement is issued.

## Software installation

Only software from the internal software catalogue may be installed. Requests for other software go through a security review that takes up to 5 working days.

## Travel with laptops

When travelling internationally, employees must keep laptops in carry-on luggage and must not connect to public Wi-Fi without the corporate VPN.
