# Catering Guidelines for Workshops and Events

## Budget

The catering budget for a customer workshop is INR 900 per attendee per day for lunch and two refreshment breaks. Internal events are capped at INR 650 per attendee per day. Alcohol may not be expensed under this budget.

## On-site catering

Where a venue has an in-house kitchen, on-site catering is the default. Riverside Hall offers a fixed working-lunch menu with vegetarian, vegan and Jain options. Hinjewadi Convention Suites includes a working lunch and two tea breaks in its per-attendee rate.

## External catering

External caterers may only be used at venues without an in-house kitchen, such as Baner Tech Hub. Only caterers listed in the approved vendor directory may be engaged. External caterers must hold a valid FSSAI licence and must deliver at least 45 minutes before the service time.

## Dietary requirements

Organisers must collect dietary requirements from attendees at least 5 working days before the event. At least 30 percent of meals must be vegetarian. Allergen labelling is mandatory for all buffet items.

## Ordering deadlines

Final headcounts must be confirmed with the caterer 48 hours before the event. Orders may be reduced by up to 10 percent after that point without charge.
