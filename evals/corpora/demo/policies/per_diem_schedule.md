# International Per Diem Schedule

Per diem covers meals and incidental expenses on international trips. It is paid per full day of travel; the day of departure and the day of return are paid at 75 percent.

## Asia-Pacific

Singapore: USD 95 per day, hotel limit USD 260 per night.
Seoul: USD 90 per day, hotel limit USD 240 per night.
Tokyo: USD 100 per day, hotel limit USD 270 per night.

## Europe

London: GBP 85 per day, hotel limit GBP 230 per night.
Berlin: EUR 80 per day, hotel limit EUR 190 per night.

## North America

San Francisco: USD 110 per day, hotel limit USD 340 per night.
New York: USD 115 per day, hotel limit USD 360 per night.

## Rules

Per diem is not paid for days on which meals are provided by the host or the conference. Per diem amounts do not require receipts, but hotel stays always do.
