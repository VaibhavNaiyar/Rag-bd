# Travel Reimbursement Policy

This policy sets out how employees are reimbursed for business travel.

## Standard reimbursement rule

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable, pre-approved business travel expenses. Every trip must have a travel request approved by the employee's line manager before any booking is made. Claims must be submitted in the expense system within 30 days of the trip ending, with an itemised receipt for every expense above INR 500.

Flights, rail and hotel stays must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is the standard for all flights under 6 hours.

## Domestic travel

For domestic trips, hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 7,500 per night in metro cities and INR 5,000 per night elsewhere. Local transport by taxi or app-based cab is reimbursed at actual cost.

## International travel

International trips require approval from both the line manager and the department head before booking. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the city limits in the international per diem schedule.

Foreign currency expenses must be supported by original receipts, and each receipt must be verified against the card or forex statement before reimbursement. Claims are reimbursed in INR at the exchange rate on the date of the transaction.

Travel insurance is mandatory for international travel and is arranged through the corporate travel portal.

## Late booking exception

A booking made after the travel has already taken place is a late booking. Late bookings are not reimbursed under the standard rule. An exception may be granted only with written approval from a senior director, and the claim must include a justification for why the booking could not be made in advance.

Late-booked international travel additionally requires the finance controller to verify the foreign currency receipts before the exception is processed.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Personal entertainment, minibar charges, traffic fines and upgrades not approved in advance are never reimbursed.
