# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 10 unused days may be carried into the next year.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Other parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Applying for leave

Leave must be applied for in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies.
