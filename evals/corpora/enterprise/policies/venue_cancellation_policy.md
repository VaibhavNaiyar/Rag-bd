# Venue Cancellation and Refund Policy

This policy applies to every venue booked through the Events Desk, including Riverside Hall, Baner Tech Hub and Hinjewadi Convention Suites.

## Cancellation windows

Cancellations made 15 or more calendar days before the event receive a full refund of the room charge.

Cancellations made between 7 and 14 calendar days before the event receive a 50 percent refund of the room charge.

Cancellations made fewer than 7 calendar days before the event are not refunded. The full room charge is billed to the requesting cost centre.

## Rescheduling

An event may be rescheduled once without penalty if the new date is within 60 days of the original date and the request is made at least 7 calendar days before the original date. A second reschedule is treated as a cancellation.

## Catering cancellations

Catering orders follow the caterer's own terms, not this policy. In-house catering at Riverside Hall must be cancelled at least 72 hours before the event; after that, 100 percent of the catering order is charged.

## Force majeure

Cancellations caused by government orders, severe weather warnings or venue closures are fully refunded regardless of timing. The Events Desk must record the reason on the booking.

## How to cancel

Cancellations must be submitted through the Events Desk portal using the booking confirmation number. Email cancellations are not accepted. The refund is credited to the original cost centre within 30 days.
