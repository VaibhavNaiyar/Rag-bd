## 6. Analysed edge-case failures

### 1. claim withheld (46 instances)

**Example** — `compound_01` (enterprise corpus)

> I need to plan a customer workshop in Pune for 30 people, and I need the cancellation policy and the catering options.

**What happened.** 2 generated claim(s) failed verification and were withheld.

**Why.** The grounding verifier could not support a generated sentence from its cited chunk, so the sentence was withheld and reported as uncertainty instead of shipped. That is the intended behaviour, but each case is worth reading: a conservative verifier costs recall of true statements.

### 2. under decomposition (35 instances)

**Example** — `compound_01` (enterprise corpus)

> I need to plan a customer workshop in Pune for 30 people, and I need the cancellation policy and the catering options.

**What happened.** 3 gold sub-intents, 2 sub-queries produced.

**Why.** Fewer sub-queries than gold sub-intents. Two different causes hide behind one symptom. On a request that bundles several asks it is a missed split: the clause splitter found no boundary, usually because the speaker used no punctuation and no conjunction that opens a new request. On an ASQA question it is not a parsing failure at all. 'Who has the highest goals in world football' has no second clause to find, and enumerating its readings (men's, women's, all-time) needs knowledge of the domain, which the deterministic splitter lacks and a language model has. This is where the model-backed decomposer earns its cost.

**Trace evidence.** `{'gold': ['Pune workshop venue with capacity for 30 people', 'venue cancellation and refund policy', 'catering options for a workshop in Pune'], 'predicted': ['customer workshop Pune 30 people cancellation policy', 'customer workshop Pune 30 people catering options'], 'merged_by_guard': 0, 'method': 'llm'}`

Other instances: asqa_compound_01, asqa_compound_02, asqa_compound_04, asqa_compound_05, asqa_compound_06, asqa_compound_07, asqa_compound_09, asqa_compound_10

### 3. late trigger (15 instances)

**Example** — `compound_04` (enterprise corpus)

> how much is the hotel limit for business trips in New York and what is the catering budget per attendee for a customer workshop

**What happened.** No provisional search fired before the speaker finished; retrieval began at the utterance end.

**Why.** The controller waits for content sufficiency and either semantic stability or a closed clause. A short utterance can end before any of those fire, so retrieval starts at the utterance end. This is the deliberate side of the trade: lowering the bar buys earlier retrieval and pays for it in searches on incomplete thoughts.

**Trace evidence.** `{'first_retrieval_ms': 11796, 'utterance_end_ms': 10511, 'decisions': [('wait', 'insufficient_content'), ('wait', 'insufficient_content'), ('wait', 'intent_unstable'), ('wait', 'intent_unstable'), ('wait', 'intent_unstable'), ('wait', 'intent_unstable'), ('retrieve', 'utterance_complete')]}`

Other instances: asqa_compound_02, asqa_compound_06, asqa_compound_07, asqa_compound_11, asqa_compound_13, asqa_compound_21, asqa_compound_23, asqa_compound_26

### 4. over fragmentation (1 instance)

**Example** — `single_03` (enterprise corpus)

> What is the per diem rate and hotel limit when travelling to London for work?

**What happened.** 2 sub-queries for 1 gold sub-intents.

**Why.** More sub-queries than gold sub-intents — the failure the merge guard and the cap exist to bound. Extra near-duplicate queries dilute the reranker's budget and buy nothing.

**Trace evidence.** `{'gold': ['What is the per diem rate and hotel limit when travelling to London for work?'], 'predicted': ['current per diem rate business travel London', 'current hotel reimbursement limit business travel London']}`
